A `(rust "Path")` clause right after the name or docstring of `defenum`,
`deftrait` or `deftype` makes the type or trait name a Rust one. This error
reports such a clause with an item after its string that the definer does not
take. `deftrait` and `deftype` take no item there, so `(rust "Copy" "Clone")`
and `(rust "f64" bare)` report it. `defenum` takes the word `bare` there, and
reports any other third item, such as a string or another word, and any fourth
item.

A later clause reports `L0340`, whatever it holds. In `deftrait`, an entry
after the clause that is a method reports `L0343` before this error.

## Erroneous example

```lisp
(defenum ordering (rust "std::cmp::Ordering" plain) less equal greater)

(defn main ()
  (match ordering/less
    (less (println "less"))
    (equal (println "equal"))
    (greater (println "greater"))))
```

## Fixes

Remove the word after the path:

```lisp
(defenum ordering (rust "std::cmp::Ordering") less equal greater)

(defn main ()
  (match ordering/less
    (less (println "less"))
    (equal (println "equal"))
    (greater (println "greater"))))
```
