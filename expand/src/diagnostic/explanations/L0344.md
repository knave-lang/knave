A `deftrait` whose body starts with a `(rust "Path")` clause, right after the
name or the docstring, names a trait that Rust defines. This error reports such
a `deftrait` whose head is a list instead of a symbol, such as `(hex-number
(display))`, or even `(hex-number)` with nothing after the name.

A `deftrait` without the clause defines a trait of the program, and its head may
be a list that names supertraits, such as `(named-area (area-of))`. An empty
head, `()`, reports `L0339` instead. `deftrait` checks a `rust` clause after
other forms, an entry after the clause that is a method, and a clause with a
second string before it checks the head, so those report `L0340`, `L0343` and
`L0341` first.

## Erroneous example

```lisp
(deftrait (hex-number (display))
  (rust "std::fmt::LowerHex")
  i64)

(def hex
  (rust "format!(\"{{:x}}\", {0})"
    (where ((a hex-number)) (fn (a) string))))

(defn main () (println (hex 255)))
```

## Fixes

Name the trait with a bare symbol:

```lisp
(deftrait hex-number
  (rust "std::fmt::LowerHex")
  i64)

(def hex
  (rust "format!(\"{{:x}}\", {0})"
    (where ((a hex-number)) (fn (a) string))))

(defn main () (println (hex 255)))
```
