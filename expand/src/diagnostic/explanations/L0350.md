`prelude/project-file` exports `defproject` and its sections, `libraries`,
`dependencies` and `dev-dependencies`, so that `knave fmt` can lay out a
project's settings by their shapes. This error reports a call of any of them in
a program, at the top level or inside a function, once a `use` has imported it.

Without the `use`, the name is unknown, `L0201`, and a reference to one of them
as a value, such as `(let (x defproject) ...)`, reports `L0304`.

## Erroneous example

```knave
(use prelude/project-file defproject)

(defproject greeter)

(defn main () (println "hello"))
```

## Fixes

Drop the `use` and the `defproject` call:

```knave
(defn main () (println "hello"))
```
