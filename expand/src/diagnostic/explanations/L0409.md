A construction with field labels, such as `(->point :x 1 :y 2)` or
`(new point (x 1) (y 2))`, builds a whole struct, so it names every declared
field. This error reports a construction that leaves out at least one field,
whether of a struct or of an enum variant with named fields. A `(defaults ...)`
clause of `defstruct` supplies the fields that it names, so a construction may
leave those out. An update such as `assoc` keeps the other fields of its base
and never reports this error.

A positional call with too few values reports `L0301` instead, and a label that
the struct does not declare reports `L0408` first.

## Erroneous example

```lisp
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64)

(defn main ()
  (let (p (->point :x 1))
    (println (:x p))))
```

## Fixes

Add the field that the construction leaves out:

```lisp
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64)

(defn main ()
  (let (p (->point :x 1 :y 0))
    (println (:x p))))
```

Or give the field a default in the struct:

```lisp
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64 (defaults :y 0))

(defn main ()
  (let (p (->point :x 1))
    (println (:x p))))
```
