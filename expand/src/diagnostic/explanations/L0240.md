An `impl` holds at most one definition for each method of its trait, and this
`impl` holds two under the same method name.

A method that the trait does not declare reports `L0241` instead.

## Erroneous example

```knave
(defstruct circle :radius f64)

(deftrait shape
  (area (fn ((ref self)) f64)))

(impl shape circle
  (area (c) (* 3.0 (:radius c) (:radius c)))
  (area (c) (* (:radius c) (:radius c))))

(defn main ()
  (println (area (->circle :radius 1.0))))
```

## Fixes

Keep one definition:

```knave
(defstruct circle :radius f64)

(deftrait shape
  (area (fn ((ref self)) f64)))

(impl shape circle
  (area (c) (* 3.0 (:radius c) (:radius c))))

(defn main ()
  (println (area (->circle :radius 1.0))))
```
