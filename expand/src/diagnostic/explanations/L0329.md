`(header-shape (twice x))` states how many leading forms of a call of `twice`
the documentation shows as its header. Its first argument is written like a call
of the form, as `(twice x)` is. This error reports a first argument of any other
kind, such as a bare symbol, a number, an empty list, or a list whose head is a
string or another list.

`header-shape` checks this before the forms that follow the shape. A head symbol
that refers to nothing where the shape is written, such as a misspelled name or
a keyword, reports `L0201`.

## Erroneous example

```knave
(use prelude/macros header-shape)

(defmacro twice (x) (template (* 2 (unquote x))))

(header-shape twice)

(defn main () (println (twice 2)))
```

## Fixes

List the leading parts of a call after the name:

```knave
(use prelude/macros header-shape)

(defmacro twice (x) (template (* 2 (unquote x))))

(header-shape (twice x))

(defn main () (println (twice 2)))
```
