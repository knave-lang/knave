`defenum`, `deftrait` and `deftype` take the name of the type they define as
their first item, either alone or at the head of a list that adds parameters
or supertraits, as in `(defenum (outcome value) ...)`. This error reports a
first item whose name position holds anything other than a symbol that is not a
keyword, such as a number, a string, a keyword like `:shape`, a list, or the
empty list `()`.

## Erroneous example

```knave
(defenum "Shapes on a page."
  shape
  circle
  square)

(defn main ()
  (match shape/circle
    (circle (println "circle"))
    (square (println "square"))))
```

## Fixes

Put the docstring after the type's name:

```knave
(defenum shape
  "Shapes on a page."
  circle
  square)

(defn main ()
  (match shape/circle
    (circle (println "circle"))
    (square (println "square"))))
```
