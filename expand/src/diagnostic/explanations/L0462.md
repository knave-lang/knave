A struct pattern or a variant pattern holds both labeled parts, written `:field
pattern`, and positional parts, such as `(point a :y b)` or `(point :x a b)`, in
either order. A labeled pattern names fields in any order and skips the rest,
while a positional pattern matches every field in field order.

The pattern reports this error before its labels are checked, so it reports it
even when a label names no field, as `(point :z a b)`, names a field twice, or
ends the pattern, as `(point a :y)`. It reports it on a variant with a
positional payload or no payload too, such as `(measured a :value b)` or `(idle
a :value b)`, where a pattern of labels alone reports `L0460`. A construction of
a variant with named fields that holds both forms, such as `(shape/labeled "box"
:size 2.0)`, reports `L0338` instead.

## Erroneous example

```knave
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64)

(defn main ()
  (match (->point :x 1 :y 2)
    ((point a :y b) (println a " " b))))
```

## Fixes

Write `:x` before `a`:

```knave
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64)

(defn main ()
  (match (->point :x 1 :y 2)
    ((point :x a :y b) (println a " " b))))
```

Or drop `:y`, and give `a` and `b` by position:

```knave
(defstruct point :x i64 :y i64)

(defn main ()
  (match (->point :x 1 :y 2)
    ((point a b) (println a " " b))))
```
